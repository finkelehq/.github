## What We Do

Finkele helps organisations understand and adapt to climate risk through live tools:

- 🚨 [**Flood Alert**](https://finkele.com/alert.html): live flood warnings across England & Scotland, ~3K stations, updated every 5 min via EA & SEPA.
- 🗺️ [**Flood Risk Map**](https://finkele.com/floodmap.html): interactive flood depth maps with risk analysis and financial loss estimates.
- 🌡️ [**Climate Dashboard**](https://finkele.com/climate.html): ESG metrics, climate trends, and AI-driven risk predictions.
- ⚡ **TERRA**: ML flood emulator trained on real solver output, milliseconds instead of hours, with a calibrated confidence interval on every prediction.
- 🛰️ **GroundVerify**: checks flood predictions against ground truth, independent Sentinel-1 satellite radar imagery of the real event, not another model's opinion.
- 🧪 **Axiom**: our open protocol for validating ML surrogates in engineering simulation. Coverage guarantees, risk detection, and a clean handoff to the full solver when a prediction can't be trusted. The test any surrogate has to pass. [Open spec →](https://github.com/finkelehq/finkele-axiom-spec)
